Our Story

A taste of home,
a long way from home.

Uwagboe's started the way the best Nigerian food always does — in a family kitchen, with a big pot, a lot of pepper and plenty of people to feed. Founders John & Funso Uwagboe brought those flavours to Scotland and built Edinburgh's first Nigerian restaurant.

Today we're a home away from home for the diaspora and a first taste of West Africa for everyone else — dine in, take away, or let us cater your owambe.
